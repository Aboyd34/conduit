Privacy Policy

Effective: March 2026  ·  conduitprotect.info  ·  admin@conduitprotect.info

1. What We Collect

Conduit collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no emails, no names, no IP logs retained. Your cryptographic identity is generated locally in your browser and never transmitted to our servers in identifiable form.

2. Local Storage

Your identity keypair, age verification token, and preferences are stored in your browser's localStorage. We have no access to this data. Clearing your browser data removes your local identity permanently.

3. Relay Behavior

The Conduit relay receives signed message packets. It does not log sender identities, IP addresses, or message content beyond the minimum required for real-time delivery. No message content is stored server-side after delivery.

4. Cookies

We use only technically necessary cookies. No advertising or tracking cookies are used. Non-essential cookies are blocked until you provide consent via the cookie banner.

5. Third Parties

Conduit does not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party. Wallet connections (if used) are handled client-side and never expose private keys to our servers.

6. Children

Conduit is restricted to users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance. Check this page periodically.

8. Contact

Questions? Email admin@conduitprotect.info
