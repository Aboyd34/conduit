Everything you need to know about Conduit. Can't find your answer? Contact us.

IDENTITY & KEYS
What is a Signal Key?

Your Signal Key is a cryptographic keypair that acts as your identity on Conduit. There's no email, no phone number, no username tied to a real identity — just your key. It's generated in your browser and stored locally.

Where is my Signal Key stored?

Your Signal Key is stored in your browser's localStorage. It never leaves your device and is never sent to our servers. Think of it like a password that only exists on your machine.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Your Signal Key will be deleted. This is permanent — we cannot recover it. Before clearing your browser, export your key from the Settings page and store it somewhere safe (like a password manager or encrypted file).

Can I use Conduit on multiple devices?

Yes. Export your Signal Key from one device and import it on another. Your identity and post history travel with the key.

Can I recover my account if I lose my key?

No. There is no account recovery. This is by design — Conduit has no central authority that can reset your identity. Guard your key like a private password. Back it up.

PRIVACY & SAFETY
Does Conduit store my posts?

Posts are stored locally in your browser's localStorage. They are not stored on a central server. If you clear your browser data, your post history is gone.

Can Conduit see my messages?

No. Direct signals (DMs) are stored only in your browser and the recipient's browser. We have no access to message content.

Is Conduit anonymous?

Your identity on Conduit is your fingerprint derived from your Signal Key — not your real name. However, your IP address may be visible to our infrastructure. Use a VPN for maximum anonymity.

Who can see my posts?

Posts in public rooms are visible to all users in that room. Ephemeral DMs are never stored and disappear when the session ends.

AETH TOKEN
What is AETH?

AETH (Aether) is Conduit's platform utility token. Holding AETH unlocks premium features including the #aether room, advanced moderation tools, and future perks.

How do I get AETH?

AETH is available through the Airdrop event. Visit the Airdrop page to see current distribution phases and requirements.

What blockchain is AETH on?

AETH contract and chain details will be published at launch. Check the Airdrop page for the latest info.

Is AETH an investment?

No. AETH is a utility token for use within the Conduit platform. It is not a security or investment vehicle. Do not purchase AETH expecting financial returns.

PLATFORM
Is Conduit free to use?

Yes. Core features are free. Premium features require AETH. There are no subscription fees.

What devices does Conduit support?

Conduit is a Progressive Web App (PWA) — it works on any modern browser on desktop or mobile. You can install it to your home screen for an app-like experience.

How do I install Conduit on my phone?

Visit conduitprotect.info in your mobile browser. Tap the install banner that appears at the bottom, or use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" option.

How do I report a user or post?

Tap the flag icon on any post to report it. Our moderation system reviews all flagged content. For urgent issues, email admin@conduitprotect.info.
